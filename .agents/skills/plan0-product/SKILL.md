Use this skill for PLAN:0 product behavior, content, backend models or APIs,
and flows that collect or present preparedness information.

Read [the product brief](references/product-brief.md) before making a change
that affects product scope, user journeys, or user-facing content.

Keep recommendations specific to the household's declared situation, resources,
needs, location, and selected hazard. Do not replace official emergency
instructions, make safety guarantees, or fabricate live shelter, incident, or
authority information. Make the source and freshness of any future live data
clear in the feature that presents it.

Preserve the distinction between ordinary preparedness and rapid crisis mode:
the former supports planning and maintenance; the latter prioritizes immediate
access to the household's essential information and instructions. If a task
requires a policy decision not established by the brief, ask for direction
instead of inventing it.
