# PLAN:0 product brief

PLAN:0 is a responsive web PWA for families and households preparing for crises
in a personalized, practical way. It is not a replacement for emergency
services, local authorities, or official real-time alerts.

## Supported product domains

- Household profile: household members, relevant needs, resources, and place of
  residence.
- Personal emergency plan: recommendations tailored to the household rather
  than a generic checklist.
- Important information: contacts, locations, meeting places, roles, and
  communication plans for households whose members may be in different places.
- Readiness: progress and gaps in a household's preparation.
- Local context: shelters and other important nearby points, presented with
  explicit source and freshness information when live data exists.
- Scenarios: preparation and action guidance for hazards such as flooding,
  blackout, fire, and evacuation.
- Rapid crisis mode: direct access to the information and instructions a
  household needs most urgently.

## Product rules

- Tailoring may use only information the household has supplied or a clearly
  identified trusted source. Do not infer sensitive needs or circumstances.
- Design for a distributed household: each member can have a role, a contact
  path, and an agreed meeting place.
- Distinguish preparation from active-event instructions. During an active
  emergency, prominently defer to official local instructions and emergency
  services.
- Do not claim a shelter is open, safe, available, or suitable without a
  sourced and current basis for that claim.
- Crisis content must be understandable under stress: concise, ordered,
  actionable, and available without color-only cues.
